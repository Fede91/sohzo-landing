# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Intellectually passive comfort-zone readers — people whose reading life has quietly narrowed into familiar topics, social feeds, or nothing at all. They haven't decided they're "not curious"; they just haven't found a way into things they don't already know. They're open to being surprised but would never seek out a philosophy essay on their own. Sohzo meets them in the morning before the day closes off.

## Product Purpose

A daily iOS app that delivers one carefully crafted short piece each morning across twelve knowledge domains. It makes cross-disciplinary discovery frictionless — one piece, every day, from a world the user wouldn't have chosen for themselves. Success looks like a reader who finishes a piece on the history of cartography and thinks: "I didn't know I cared about that."

## Brand Personality

Precise, Revealing, Unhurried. The antithesis of the feed. Not playful, not academic. Like a very good magazine editor who trusts readers to be interested in things they haven't tried yet — no streak anxiety, no gamification, no noise. Just a quiet habit that pays off slowly.

## Anti-references

- **Duolingo**: gamified, streak-driven, cartoon mascot, infantilizing reward loops — Sohzo is for adults and trusts them.
- **Medium**: blogger-tone, recommendation engine churn, clutter, byline-first culture — Sohzo foregrounds the piece, not the author.
- **Facebook / Instagram / X / Threads**: infinite scroll, engagement metrics, dopamine loop UI, social comparison — Sohzo is a closed container, not a feed.

## Design Principles

1. **Quiet confidence over persuasion** — no badges, urgency copy, or streak mechanics. The work earns the habit.
2. **The piece is the product** — every design choice defers to the reading experience. The interface should feel like a well-set page.
3. **Curation is the feature** — twelve worlds, one at a time, removes choice paralysis. No browsing, no recommendations, no algorithm.
4. **Morning-appropriate** — calm, low-friction at peak attention. Nothing competes with the content.
5. **Breadth without superficiality** — different domains, same depth and editorial care in each.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Reduced-motion already handled via `prefers-reduced-motion` in the stylesheet. No specific additional requirements stated.
